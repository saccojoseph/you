# Take a tour of YOU

This is a 90-second walkthrough of the [interactive demo](https://you-relationship-memory.saccojoseph961968.chatgpt.site/). It uses fictional data throughout. No account connection, device reading, ticket booking, or production AI service is active.

1. On the welcome screen, choose **Explore the demo**. The sample persona is Alex. Choose **Begin setup** later if you want to see the engram questionnaire and assistant selection.
2. On **For you**, open **Why this?** on a suggestion. YOU shows the sample evidence and its uncertainty. Dismiss a suggestion or mark the possible commitment handled to see the page react.
3. Open **Memory**. Compare a known preference, Mike's inferred interest in golf, and an unknown date. Open a memory to inspect its source, confidence, and agent-availability setting.
4. Open **People** and select Mike. Explore his profile, then open **Relationships** to see how people are connected.
5. In **Ask YOU**, ask “Why do you think Mike likes golf?” Then ask “What don't you know yet?” The answers use visible sample memory and show sources rather than inventing facts.
6. Open **Connections** to see possible sources and agent harnesses. They are placeholders. In **Privacy & access**, review the controls, export the browser's demo data, or reset it.

Your edits stay in your browser's local storage. If you have used an older version of the prototype, this tour starts from a new fictional seed set; the app does not upload or migrate the old browser data. Please avoid entering real sensitive information in this prototype.
